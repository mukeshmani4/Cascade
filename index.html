Shipping software is building a house. An accessibility miss is a wall in the wrong place — the tool you need depends on when you notice it.

    Design

    The blueprint. A wrong wall is an eraser fix.

  2. 2

    Cost to fix: 2 / 4

    The inspection fails. You tear open the walls.

    Cost to fix: 3 / 4

  4. 4
